
# 👟✨ Subazz Shoe Mart

### 🛍️ **Project Overview**

**Subazz Shoe Mart** is a modern **full-stack e-commerce web application** designed to provide a smooth, secure, and user-friendly online shopping experience for footwear. The platform allows users to explore and purchase a wide variety of shoes through different categories such as **Men, Women, Kids, Sports, Running, Casual, and Formal** footwear.

### 🔐 **User Authentication**

Users can securely **Sign in with Google/Gmail** and access personalized features such as **Wishlist, Watchlist, Cart, Orders, and Profile**. The authentication system is designed using secure **Google OAuth**, without storing users' Gmail passwords.

### 👟 **Product Experience**

Each shoe has a dedicated **Product Details Page** where users can view multiple images, product name, brand, price, discount, description, available sizes, colors, ratings, reviews, and stock availability. Users can select their preferred size and quantity and **Add to Cart** or **Buy Now**.

### ❤️ **Wishlist & Sharing**

Users can **Like ❤️ and save shoes** to their personal Wishlist/Watchlist for future purchases. A **Share 🔗** feature allows users to share individual product links through WhatsApp, social media, email, or supported device sharing.

### 🛒 **Shopping & Orders**

The application includes a complete **Shopping Cart 🛒, Checkout 💳, Address Management 📍, Order History 📦, and User Dashboard 👤**. Users can easily manage their purchases and track their order information.

### ⚙️ **Admin Dashboard**

An **Admin Dashboard 🛠️** allows administrators to manage products, categories, prices, discounts, inventory, users, reviews, and orders efficiently.

### 💻 **Technology Stack**

The project is developed using **React.js, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Axios, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB**, with **REST APIs** connecting the frontend and backend.

### 🚀 **Project Goal**

The main goal of **Subazz Shoe Mart** is to create a **professional, responsive, scalable, and production-ready online shoe shopping platform** that delivers a simple, secure, and engaging shopping experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
